The publication and talk bylines resolve canonical author slugs to these profiles, preserving the original author names, order, and contribution notes. Unprofiled coauthors remain credited by their full names. Each profile lists its shared publications, and each paper's structured data includes every author in order. Current profile affiliations are biographical context and do not replace historical affiliations in the original papers.

Hidden profiles retain their source data and photographs for restoration. Their author term pages set `profile: false` and `build.render: never` / `build.list: never`; these settings suppress the pages, profile links, portraits, and article bio cards while keeping plain author names in citations and structured data. To restore one, remove `profile: false`, set `build.render: always`, and remove the `build.list: never` override.

Xuanli Lin's existing biography was converted to third person without changing its substantive information. His existing portrait is retained. Photo provenance is not a claim of an open license; third-party photographs remain attributable to their original sources.

## Public profile links

Author links are ordered consistently: Webpage, Email, Google Scholar, LinkedIn, GitHub, ORCID, then other verified person profiles. A webpage link points to a personal homepage or institutional person page; papers and news articles are not used as biography outlinks. Categories without a verified person-specific link are omitted. Publication sources remain in these provenance notes.

The per-author source notes above document how each added link was matched to the correct person. Existing Xuanli Lin links are retained in the same order, his public `kazumi@asu.edu` email comes from the site's contact section, and his existing ORCID identifier now uses its public profile URL.
